Verified — leave it alone

A document + its producer. You can point to the source. It needs no further work.

Assumed — defend or replace

A basis + a date. A defensible estimate, not a fact. Show its basis, or replace it with evidence.

Open — chase it

Named + tracked. No acceptable evidence yet. Not zero, not a guess — a finding to close.
