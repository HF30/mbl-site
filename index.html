One house in. Six homes out.

MBL Developments replaces tired single-family houses with new six-unit rental buildings in Toronto’s as-of-right sixplex wards — data-driven acquisition, CMHC-financed, built to hold for decades.

What we do

Toronto quietly made its biggest zoning change in generations: six rental units, as-of-right, on regular residential lots in nine wards. We built a machine for exactly that opportunity.

A specific buy box

Single-family lots in Toronto’s nine as-of-right sixplex wards, replaced with five- and six-unit purpose-built rentals. No rezoning. Every ward and property carries its own maximum bid — the formula decides, not the address.

Evidence, not asking prices

We underwrite against thousands of closed leases and sales — never listings. Rents at closed unit comps, values at a cap rate above every trade in our tracked 2025–26 east-end closed set.

Institutional financing

Projects designed to CMHC MLI Select’s 100+ point tier from the first sketch — energy and affordability engineered in, unlocking MLI Select’s strongest tier of leverage and amortization for rental housing — subject to lender underwriting.

A proven delivery machine

Our delivery team has delivered six CMHC multiplex builds, with an in-house MLI Select energy practice and a construction partner building for four decades — on-budget, on-schedule, fixed-price.
